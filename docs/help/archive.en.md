# Archiving and finding chats and tasks

The chats and tasks **in progress** for each duty live in the Chats card of the side panel (the panel icon at the far top right of the window opens and closes it; it is open by default on position, duty and task pages): the Chats card lists that duty’s chats (on a position page, all of them grouped by duty). When nobody touches one for a few days, it is tucked away (archived) so the list only shows what is active. Archiving **deletes nothing**: messages, summary, memory and card history all stay, and you can bring it back any time.

## The “+” buttons in the sidebar

- **“+” next to the Positions heading**: create a position — give it a name, tick the duties; it is assigned to you and opened.
- **“+” on a position row** (shows on hover): add duties to that position.
- **“+” on a duty row** (shows on hover): opens that duty with a “New chat” box ready in the Chats card — type one line and press Enter. “+ New chat” at the top of the Chats card does the same.

Only the company owner can create positions and add duties; other people don’t see the first two “+”.

## The marks before a chat

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Spinner | The agent is working on it |
| Amber dot | Waiting for you: a card to approve, or finished work to look at |
| No mark | Open, waiting for the next step |

When something is waiting for you, an amber dot shows after the Chats card title, and on the panel icon at the top right too. The cards themselves are under “Needs you” on the position page.

## When things are archived

- By default after **3 days** without activity. Change it in Settings → Running tasks → Auto-archive (1–30 days, or never).
- **Never** archived automatically: anything running, anything with a card waiting for you.
- Any new activity — you reply, a schedule fires, the agent runs again — brings it **back to the list by itself**.

## Archiving by hand

Don’t want to wait three days? In the Chats card, hover the item and click “⋯” → “Archive”; the item page has an “Archive” button at the top too. A bar “Archived … · Undo” appears at the bottom of the sidebar — click Undo to bring it back.

Anything running, or with a card waiting for your approval, can’t be archived; the button is greyed out and the question mark next to it says why.

## Finding something again

1. **By duty**: in the Chats card click “Show more”; under each duty, “Archived (n)” opens a list you can filter by time, position and duty. Each row has “Restore”.
2. **Search**: the search box on that list, or ⌘K. Titles, summaries and message text all count. ⌘K marks archived results.
3. **Ask AI**: if you only remember roughly, describe it — e.g. “bring back the chat about samples with that US creator last week”:
   - say it in **Assistant**;
   - or type it in **⌘K** and pick the last item, “Ask AI to find”;
   - or use the box at the bottom of the Archived list.

   AI shows the closest few as cards. **Only the card you click is restored** — AI never restores anything by itself, and never a batch at once.

## Tips

- An archived item still opens in full; there is a “Put back” button at the top.
- AI looks at titles, summaries, people and message text, and understands “last week”, “yesterday”, “3 days ago”.
