# Agents Workshop models and credits

Don't want to sign up with each provider and copy keys around? Use Agents Workshop's models: **no key, no other sign-ups** — link an Agents Workshop account once, pay in credits, and switch back to your own keys any time. New accounts get 5 credits.

Not linking is fine too — your own keys go direct from this computer and cost nothing here: they never pass through us.

## Link your account (setup step ① / Settings → Account & credits)

1. No account yet? Pick "Sign up": enter your name, email and a password, accept the terms, then type the 6-digit code from your inbox.
2. Already have one? Pick "Sign in" with an email code (default) or your password. Once verified it shows "Linked" and the credits you received.
3. If you chose this in setup, we finish three things for you: turn on Agents Workshop models, switch capabilities to "use Agents Workshop's", and put the official data API first for creator channels.

If the network is down it says it couldn't reach Agents Workshop cloud — try again, or look around with demo data first.

## Turn the model on / off in Settings

1. Link your Agents Workshop account in [Settings → Account & credits](/settings/credits).
2. Back in "Settings → Models", click "Enable" on the "Agents Workshop cloud (credits)" card.
3. Pick a model (the list comes from the cloud, sorted by credit price).
4. Use it — what each call costs is under "Account & credits" (the card also shows this month's usage and balance).
5. To go back to your own key, remove / disable this entry any time: after that this machine no longer uses credits for models; what was charged isn't refunded and nothing more is charged.

## How credits work

- **1 credit = ¥1**. Models run on our cloud; tokens are converted into credits.
- Purchased credits **never expire**; promotional ones reset each period; expiring credits are used first.
- Top up by picking a tier: US$1 = 7 credits; payment happens on Stripe's page — we never touch your card number.
- Check your balance any time: click your name at the bottom left — the first line of the menu is your credits, with "Top up" next to it. When it runs low a small dot appears on your avatar, and the card warns you too. The price list is the final price, no extra service fee; before linking you see the built-in list, and the one shown after linking counts.
- Owners see usage for the whole organisation, members only their own workspace. Who provides each capability (your key or Agents Workshop's) is set under "Settings → Models" and "Settings → Data sources".

## The three things you pay for

| Block | What | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Data APIs | Platform fees and quota we pay for you (creator lookups, social scraping, web pages, transcription) | per call / page / minute |
| AI usage | Models | per 1K tokens |
| Add-on services | Creator marketing, customer service add-ons | monthly, 30 credits / month each |

Image generation through the official API is charged per image; failures cost nothing.

## Creator marketing add-on

It isn't a backup: your creator library lives both locally and in the cloud, editable on either side and synced both ways — the foundation for creator marketing that doesn't depend on this one machine. 30 credits / month (= ¥30), cancel any time (runs to the end of the period, no data touched). If a payment fails, sync pauses and no data is touched on either side; it resumes once you top up.
