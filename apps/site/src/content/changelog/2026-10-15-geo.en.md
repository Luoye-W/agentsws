---
date: 2026-10-15
title: AI answer tracking
summary: Each week the Web Operations role asks AIs like ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude about your category, and records whether you are mentioned and which sites they cite.
---

- Which questions to track, and how many, starts as a suggestion from your brand profile; you just confirm.
- A dashboard at the top of the Content & Search page: mention-rate trends, every question's answer from every AI, the most-cited sites, and you versus competitors.
- Every answer is kept in full with its citations, so you can see what changed since last week.
