title: Missing something? Fill it in right there
summary: If a task needs a connection or a setting you don't have yet, a card pops up on the spot; fill it in and the original task carries on.
---

- Connection and settings cards appear in your card feed, in the task and in the side panel. No trip to a settings page.
- The AI says what's missing, why it needs it and what it suggests; the same gap is only asked about once.
- Connecting on the Connections page counts too: the card closes itself and the work resumes.
