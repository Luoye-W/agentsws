title: Inbox rules in one sentence, and follow-ups for unanswered email
summary: Say “tag payout notices, log them to the sheet, don’t bother me” and it tries the rule on 30 days of mail before creating it. No reply to your email in 3 working days? A follow-up card.
---

- Rules get a trial run on the last 30 days of mail; you see what would match and the rule only goes live when you click Create. The rule list shows how often each one matched, and deletes can be undone.
- Follow-ups come with a drafted nudge. If they named a date, it waits until then. Once they reply, the card disappears.
- Reply drafts are written in the sender's language and in your brand voice.
