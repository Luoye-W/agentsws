title: A refreshed interface
summary: The sidebar is now Home, Assistant, Inbox, and Calendar & to-dos; Home shows your day in four blocks; every role page keeps a board column on the right. Feedback sits in the bottom-left corner.
---

- Home, top to bottom: a greeting and one-line summary, the inbox and today's to-dos, key numbers, and every role. One box at the bottom to hand over anything else.
- Role pages: what needs you on top, chats, reports, plans and connections on the right, the input box at the bottom. Reports open with three lines of conclusions and say which connection is missing when a number can't be read.
- Settings are grouped into Personal, AI, Brand, This computer and Team; account connections are one searchable line per service.
- Feedback in the bottom-left corner: attach a screenshot and diagnostics, no sign-in needed, and our reply arrives in the bell.
