---
date: 2026-10-15
title: Say it, and it's set up
summary: Weekly reports and logging to your own spreadsheet are no longer fixed features. Tell a role in a sentence and it configures itself.
---

- Want a report every Friday? Say so and it sets up a scheduled task; want a different format, say that too.
- Want sent messages copied to your own spreadsheet? It reads the headers, maps the columns and shows you a sample row on a card. Nothing is written until you click "Keep".
- Reports can be copied and exported, and every version is kept.
