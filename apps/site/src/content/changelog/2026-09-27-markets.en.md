title: Target markets
summary: Paste your website and it works out which countries you sell to.
---

- During setup it reads your site’s country switcher, language folders and shipping policy to infer your markets, and shows where each conclusion came from. If it can’t tell, it leaves the field empty for you.
- Once your store is connected it checks again against your shipping zones and tells you what changed. Anything you edited yourself is left alone.
- Search visibility and AI answer checks run per market from this list.
