title: Web Operations gets an SEO & GEO duty
summary: A technical health check every Monday across seven areas, with fixes handed straight to Site Building. Alerts now live in the bell, urgent ones in red.
---

- Seven areas: indexing, canonicals, redirects and broken links, sitemaps, hreflang, structured data and speed. It also watches for drops after a redesign, your own pages competing for the same keyword, and sorts traffic drops by likely cause.
- The old “Content & search” duty is back to “Content & blog” and only writes; before publishing it checks citations, the target-market version and how easily AI can quote the page.
- Alerts all go to the bell under their own heading: urgent ones on top in red, one click to the role or task.
