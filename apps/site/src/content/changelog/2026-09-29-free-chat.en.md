- "Free chat" now sits at the top of the left rail: streaming answers, stop, regenerate, copy.
- Turn on web search and sources come back as links; turn on "Answer from company docs" and it reads only your knowledge base, with citations.
- When the chat turns into real work, "Hand to a role" passes the last question and answer to the right role.
- Chat history stays on your computer.
