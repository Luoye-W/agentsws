title: Sign-up and sign-in
summary: Sign up with your name, email and a password; verify your email and get 10 free credits. Sign in with an email code or your password.

- You agree to the Terms at sign-up; once your email is verified, 10 credits land right away and last 90 days.
- Sign in with an email code by default, or with your password; you can reset a forgotten password yourself.
- The desktop app and the web account page share the same account.
