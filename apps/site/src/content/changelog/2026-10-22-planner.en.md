date: 2026-10-22
title: Calendar and to-dos on one page
summary: Add a to-do in one sentence; for anything without a date, the AI finds a slot around your working hours, and nothing moves until you accept.
---

- Three kinds of to-do: date only, with a time, and not scheduled yet. Drag one onto the calendar and it changes kind to match.
- “Find me a slot”: the AI draws dashed suggestions first, and only books what you accept. Set your working hours, weekends, buffers around live streams and lunch once in Settings → General → My schedule.
- “This week” runs Monday to Sunday; anything unfinished from last week rolls over, marked as carried over.
