date: 2026-09-29
title: The B2B role
summary: Inquiries are graded four ways and likely scams are flagged red; quotes over a rep’s limit go to the lead.
---

- Every new inquiry gets a grade: real buyer, price shopping, sample-hunting, or likely scam. Likely scams are flagged and never answered automatically.
- The rest get a drafted first reply as a card for you to approve. WhatsApp inquiries take the same path.
- Quotes come out as a PDF, with terms taken from the quote version and your fact cards. Anything over a rep’s limit goes to the lead.
- When a rep leaves or changes territory, a handover card is created; ownership only moves once it is approved.
