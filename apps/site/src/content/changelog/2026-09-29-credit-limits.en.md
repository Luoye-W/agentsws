title: One company balance, monthly limits per member and role
summary: The whole company shares one balance; the lead decides how much each person and role can spend per month.
---

- The company shares one credit balance instead of topping up per person.
- Set a monthly limit for each member and each role. At 80% the page warns you; at 100% the lead and admins get a notice.
- Going over only blocks that one request, and says which limit ran out: company, personal or role.
