title: "Ad creative: find the weak ones, write new ones"
summary: Upload a Google or Meta export; the Paid Ads role finds the weak ads, writes new headlines and descriptions, and builds an upload sheet in the official format.
---

- Weak ads are picked against the median of the same export; anything without enough data is listed separately instead of guessed at.
- New headlines and descriptions are checked locally for length and banned words; images can go to the Design role in a batch.
- Upload sheets follow each platform's official bulk-upload template, column for column.
