# Refund Policy

This policy explains how credit top-ups are refunded and how chargebacks are handled. The operator is **{{OPERATOR_LEGAL_NAME}}**.

The short version: **unspent purchased credits can be refunded; credits you've already spent can't — and we never come after you for what you've already used.**

## 1. What can be refunded

1. The **unspent part of purchased credits** can be refunded.
2. Refunds are worked out **per payment**: whatever share of a payment's credits is left is refunded in the same proportion. For example, if you paid USD 100 for 700 credits and have used 140, the remaining 560 credits mean a refund of USD 80.
3. If you've made several top-ups, the most recent one is refunded first by default; you can name a specific one when you ask.

## 2. What can't be refunded

1. **Credits already spent** — they've already become model calls, data lookups or add-on services.
2. **Free credits** (sign-up, promotions, etc.) — they were free, so they can't be refunded or exchanged for cash.
3. **The current period of an add-on service** — cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period, which isn't refunded; nothing is charged from the next period on.

## 3. How to ask for a refund

1. Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with your account email, the top-up you want refunded (order ID, or date and amount) and a short reason.
2. Once we've checked it, we refund to the original payment method in the original currency. When the refund is confirmed, the matching credits from that payment are removed from your balance straight away.
3. How long it takes to arrive depends on the payment provider and your card issuer — usually a few working days to a few weeks. Any fees or exchange-rate differences charged by the payment channel follow the provider's rules.

## 4. Partial refunds

If only part of a payment is refunded, we reverse **the same proportion** of that payment's credits. For example, refunding USD 20 of a USD 50 (350-credit) top-up reverses 140 credits.

## 5. Chargebacks (disputes with your bank or card issuer)

1. If something's wrong, please contact us first — we'll sort it out quickly, and it's usually much faster than a chargeback.
2. If you dispute a top-up with your bank or card issuer, we **reverse the unspent credits from that payment**. Anything already spent is only noted in our records; we won't pursue you for it.
3. While a dispute is open we may pause top-ups on the account; if the dispute is decided in favour of the payment, we restore the reversed credits.
4. For clearly abusive or repeated chargebacks we may restrict or close the account under the [Terms of Service](/terms/).

## 6. When the balance can't cover a reversal

If some of a payment's credits have already been used by the time of a refund or chargeback, we only reverse what's left. Your balance never goes negative, and you can keep using the open-source software.

## 7. Where the law gives you more

If the law where you live gives you stronger refund rights (such as a statutory right of withdrawal), the law prevails.
