## Hire by role

Short on people? Add a role. Each one owns its lane and keeps its own memory; anything that needs your call becomes a card first.

| Role | What it does | Cards it hands you |
|---|---|---|
| Customer Care | Email, site chat, Amazon messages, drafted for you to send | Replies · Refunds / reships · Address changes · Follow-ups |
| Creator Marketing | Find and score creators, pitch, follow through | Pitches · Quotes and terms · Content review |
| Social Media | Plan posts, reply; checks your Reddit inbox and subreddits daily | Posts · Comment replies · Reddit replies (filled in by the extension, you hit send) |
| Web Operations | Products, promos, email; a weekly SEO & GEO health check, and how AI answers about your category | Price / promo changes · Email campaigns · Fulfilment · Health-check fixes |
| Amazon Operations | Shows up only if you sell on Amazon: listing check-ups, keyword gaps, competitor ASIN watch | Competitor watch plan · Image requests to Design |
| Paid Ads | Google and Meta: watch spend, write new copy, preview every change; six more platforms through their exported reports | Stop-loss pauses · New headlines and descriptions · Upload sheets (preview first) |
| Site Building | Builds pages and switches themes on Shopify, and shows you first | Theme choice · Page edits · Launch checklist |
| B2B | Grade inquiries, quote within your limits, follow orders through | First replies · Quotes (PDF) · Sample notices · Rep handovers |
| Public Relations | Press releases, forum presence, mentions and early warnings | Press pitches · Forum replies · Negative-mention alerts |
| Design | On-brand images for your site, Amazon, social and ads | Images · Ad creative in batches |

Need something else? Create your own role on the Company page, and rename any role to match how your company talks. → [What each role does](https://agentsws.com/en/roles/)

## Open it every day

- **Inbox** — Cards from every role and the emails only you can decide on, in one queue. Scan the list or go one at a time from the keyboard.
- **Calendar & to-dos** — Add a to-do in one sentence; for anything without a date, the AI finds a slot around your working hours, and nothing moves until you accept.
- **Home** — How many things wait for you, today’s to-dos, yesterday’s sales, and what each role is doing.

## Overseas content: found, and opened

A lot of overseas sites can’t be reached through DeepSeek’s built-in search, including many of the ones that matter most to brands selling abroad. So the Workshop has its own: search runs through our own data service and returns Google / Bing results from overseas; pages that need a login or a click are opened in a browser.

<details>
<summary><b>Three ways to drive a browser</b></summary>

- **Our own browser extension** — Agents Workshop Assistant is our own Chrome extension, for Reddit posting and creator research with your own logins. It fills replies into the thread you have open, and you press send.
- **Tencent’s open-source BrowserSkill** — Automation in the Chrome or Edge you use every day. The AI works in a separate window and asks before touching tabs you already have open; the local helper is pinned to a fixed version and checksum.
- **Tuned for ego lite** — A browser built for AI automation; if you have it installed, pick it in Settings and every step shows up in the run log.

</details>

## Tell it in a few sentences. It sets itself up.

No admin panels to learn, no workflows to draw. If something’s missing, it asks you in the chat.

<details>
<summary><b>Connect & set up</b> · one-click connections · fills gaps on the spot · say it and it’s set up</summary>

- **Connect your platforms in one click** — Shopify, Amazon, Google / Meta Ads, Reddit…: through the accounts already signed in to in your browser, or one click to authorize. No developer apps, no terminal.
- **Fills gaps on the spot** — Missing a connection or a setting? A card pops up in the chat; fill it in and the work carries on.
- **Say it, and it’s set up** — A weekly report, logging to your own spreadsheet: just tell it. No waiting for us to ship a feature.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Email & replies</b> · inbox rules in one sentence · follow-ups for unanswered email · Reddit replies filled in for you</summary>

- **Inbox rules in one sentence** — “Tag payout notices, log them to the sheet, don’t bother me.” It tries the rule on the last 30 days of mail first; it only goes live when you click Create.
- **It remembers who hasn’t replied** — No reply to your email in 3 working days? A follow-up card with the nudge already drafted; once they reply, the card disappears.
- **Reddit replies filled in by the extension** — Drafts go to the browser extension, which fills them into the thread you have open; you press send.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Schedule</b> · AI finds free slots · shows up on schedule</summary>

- **AI finds free slots** — Around the hours you set (working hours, weekends, live streams, lunch); nothing moves until you accept. Unfinished to-dos from last week roll into this week.
- **Shows up on schedule** — Daily checks and weekly reports, right on time.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Data & search</b> · answers as charts · weekly SEO & GEO check · what AI says about your category</summary>

- **Answers come as charts** — Ask about your numbers and get stat cards, charts and tables; numbers it can’t read are marked with the missing connection.
- **Weekly SEO & GEO health check** — Every Monday: indexing, canonicals, broken links, sitemaps, hreflang, structured data and speed. Fixes go to the Site Building role.
- **What AI says about your category** — Do ChatGPT and Perplexity mention you? Wrong names, prices and selling points go on a list and get re-checked after a fix.

## AI proposes. You decide. Always.

Anything that goes out, costs money or changes something becomes a card in your inbox first, with reasons and sources. You choose: send, edit, or drop.

Refunds, reships, price changes and publishing are always human-approved. No approval rate unlocks autopilot.

## Your business stays on your computer

- **Double-click to install** — Mac and Windows. No Docker, no command line.
- **Your keys stay yours** — Store and mailbox passwords go into a native form and an encrypted vault on this computer; never seen by the AI, never logged.
- **Cloud is opt-in** — Data only goes to the cloud when you turn on a cloud service or run a long task there: just the part that’s needed, exportable and deletable.
- **Model down? Everything stops** — No half-baked sends when the AI can’t be reached.

<details>
<summary>What do the anonymous usage stats include?</summary>

Which features were used and which buttons were clicked, with nothing about your store, customers or orders. On by default during the beta; one switch in Settings → Data & privacy turns them off.

</details>

<details>
<summary>For one person, a team, or the whole company</summary>

- **Solo (default)** — One person running several brands; every role is yours, and nothing goes out until you approve it.
- **Team** — Colleagues side by side: everyone runs their own roles and nobody signs off on anybody. Hand work to a colleague; it’s theirs once they accept. Colleagues on another network connect through the team channel, billed by headcount.
- **Whole company** — The owner turns it on: one balance, a monthly cap and store access for each member, and what matters goes up the chain for approval.

Start on your own and move up when you need to. Your data comes along; nothing to reinstall.

</details>

## For developers

### Run from source

You need Node ≥ 22 and pnpm ≥ 10 (`corepack enable pnpm`). None of the steps below need a model key or any account.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Luoye-W/agentsws.git
cd agentsws
pnpm install
pnpm build

# The workstation, backed by a simulated company; listens on 127.0.0.1 only (add --port 4318 if the port is taken)
pnpm dev:demo

# The simulation loop: the simulated company runs every scenario, and all six invariants must pass (this is the CI merge gate)
pnpm simulate --tier fast --pack packs/dtc-3c-3p --scenario 'scenarios/**/*.yml' --seed 42

pnpm test
pnpm lint
```

Just want to use it? Grab the desktop app from the [download page](https://agentsws.com/en/download/) instead.
